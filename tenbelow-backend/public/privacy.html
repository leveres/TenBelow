Privacy Policy

Effective April 24, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Innovative Codeworks LLC, doing business as TenBelow, collects, uses, shares, stores, and protects information when you use the TenBelow application, marketplace, storefront tools, support channels, and related services.

1. Information we collect

Information you provide may include:

  • Name, email address, username, authentication information, profile details, and storefront information.
  • Shipping details, transaction records, order history, support messages, exchanges, and custom requests.
  • Product descriptions, photos, videos, reviews, messages, and other content you upload.
  • Seller onboarding, payout, tax-related, verification, moderation, and compliance information.

We may also collect device information, app version, diagnostics, usage activity, security logs, push notification tokens, session information, and marketplace activity. Payment card information is processed by payment providers such as Stripe; TenBelow does not directly store full payment card numbers.

2. How we use information

We use information to create and manage accounts, operate and secure the marketplace, process transactions and payouts, fulfill orders, review listings, provide support, communicate order and account updates, prevent fraud, enforce our policies, comply with law, improve TenBelow, and promote products and creators.

3. How we share information

We may share information with service providers that support payments, payouts, hosting, media storage, email, notifications, analytics, security, and customer support. Buyers and sellers may receive the order, shipping, storefront, and support information reasonably needed to complete marketplace transactions. We may also disclose information when required by law or necessary to protect users, TenBelow, or the public.

4. Public information

Storefront names, usernames, creator biographies, profile information, product listings, listing media, reviews, and promotional content may be visible to other users or the public.

5. Notifications and communications

TenBelow may send transactional and service communications, including verification messages, receipts, shipment updates, exchange notices, support messages, seller onboarding updates, and security notices. Device notification preferences can be managed in the app or system settings.

6. Data retention

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary to operate the marketplace, fulfill orders, resolve disputes, prevent fraud, satisfy accounting and tax requirements, comply with law, and maintain platform security. Account deletion may remove active access and listings while required historical, payment, order, audit, support, or compliance records are retained.

7. Your choices

You may update certain account information through the app and request account deletion through TenBelow support, subject to legally required retention. You may also manage device permissions and notifications through your device settings.

8. Data security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards designed to protect information. No transmission or storage system can be guaranteed completely secure.

9. Children’s privacy

TenBelow is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

10. Third-party services and international use

Third-party services have their own privacy practices. TenBelow is operated from the United States, and information may be processed in the United States or other jurisdictions used by our providers.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Revised versions become effective when posted through the application, website, or related services.
